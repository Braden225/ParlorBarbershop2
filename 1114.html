04Before you come

Three things worth mentioning at booking.

  1. 1

    If you want a shave.

    It holds the chair for longer than a haircut, so it has to be on the book rather than sprung on the day.

  2. 2

    If it is a first haircut.

    Children’s first cuts go better with a longer slot and no line building up behind them. Say so and the time gets set aside.

  3. 3

    If you have a barber here already.

    Ask for them by name. Somebody who already knows your growth pattern is worth waiting an extra day for.
